Important Information

This prototype extracts selectable text from PDFs and checks for selected keywords. A passed check only means that a matching keyword was found. A failed check does not establish regulatory noncompliance. Actual labeling requirements depend on the device, intended use, target market, and applicable regulations. Human regulatory review is required before any label is approved or released.
